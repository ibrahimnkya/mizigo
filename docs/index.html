Station Operator
Depot Scope

Handles all physical parcel operations at a single depot. They are the frontline staff who receive cargo, dispatch to vehicles, offload arriving shipments, and verify handovers with OTP.

Capabilities
Receive cargo at depot
Dispatch to vehicle
Offload arrivals
OTP delivery verification
Print receipts
Parcel status updates
Operational access — single depot only
Platform Agent
Partner Scope

Independent or business partners who book shipments on behalf of customers. Agents go through KYC verification and earn commissions on every booking they facilitate through the platform.

Capabilities
KYC & identity verification
Book parcels for clients
Commission tracking
Parcel status lookup
Partner access — booking & commissions only
Regular Customer
Public Access

End-users who use MiziGO to send and receive parcels. They can book door-to-door deliveries, check shipping rates with the rate calculator, and follow their parcels in real-time via the tracking portal.

Capabilities
Self-service booking
Rate calculator
Real-time tracking
SMS notifications
Public portal — no admin access

Tariff category management
